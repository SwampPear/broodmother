# ✍️ Inventor Assignment Agreement (template)

**Working copy — Proprium Labs, Inc. — Confidential** · *Prepared 2026-07-20*

> **Not filed with the provisional** (assignment recording is optional at filing and done later via the USPTO Assignment Center), but **every inventor should have a signed assignment to Proprium Labs, Inc. regardless** — this is what makes Proprium the owner of the invention and underpins the assignee/applicant entries on the [[Form AIA-14 — Application Data Sheet|ADS]] and the ownership condition in the [[Form SB-15A — Micro Entity Certification|micro-entity certification]].
>
> Part of [[Provisional #1 Filing Package]]. **This is a plain-language template, not a vetted legal instrument — have counsel produce or review the executed version.**

---

## Why this matters here

- The [[Patent Plan]] notes inventors should have signed assignments *before* filing.
- The [[Form SB-15A — Micro Entity Certification|SB/15A]] condition (4) turns on who the invention is obligated to be assigned to — a signed assignment to Proprium Labs is what makes that answer clean.
- Recording is done **after** filing via the [Assignment Center](https://assignmentcenter.uspto.gov) once the application number exists.

## Template — Assignment of Invention and Patent Rights

> **ASSIGNMENT OF INVENTION**

**Assignor (Inventor):** ______________________, residing at ______________________ ("Assignor").

**Assignee:** **Proprium Labs, Inc.**, a corporation organized under the laws of ______________, with a principal place of business at ______________________ ("Assignee").

**Recitals.** Assignor has made or contributed to an invention entitled **"Systems and Methods for Wash-Free, Label-Free DNA Sequencing by Synthesis Using Multi-Layer AC Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy and Machine-Learning Base Calling"** (the "Invention"), as described in a U.S. provisional patent application filed on or about ____-__-__ (Application No. ____________, if known).

**Assignment.** For good and valuable consideration, receipt of which is acknowledged, Assignor hereby irrevocably assigns to Assignee its successors and assigns, the entire right, title, and interest, worldwide, in and to:

1. the Invention;
2. the above-identified provisional application and **all** applications claiming priority to it — including non-provisional, continuation, divisional, continuation-in-part, reissue, PCT, and foreign counterpart applications;
3. all patents that may issue from any of the foregoing; and
4. all rights to claim priority, and all rights to sue for and recover for past, present, and future infringement.

**Further assurances.** Assignor agrees to execute all documents and take all actions reasonably requested by Assignee, at Assignee's expense, to perfect, record, and enforce the rights assigned, and appoints Assignee as attorney-in-fact to do so if Assignor is unavailable.

**Governing law.** This Assignment is governed by the laws of ______________.
