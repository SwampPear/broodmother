# SOSV SF & NY (IndieBio)

Status: DRAFT — 2026-07-25. Deep-tech / biotech accelerator; rebranded from IndieBio to SOSV SF / SOSV NY in early 2026 (same program, broader remit). Part of [[Accelerators]]; see [[Funding]]. Sources accessed 2026-07-25: [SOSV/IndieBio terms](https://slidebean.com/startup-accelerator-program/sosv-indiebio), [rebrand](https://sosv.com/indiebio-is-now-sosv-ny-sosv-sf-underscoring-sosvs-leadership-in-deep-tech/).

**One line:** the strongest *biotech-instrument narrative* accelerator fit, and it comes with wet-lab access — rolling, so apply post-pixel.

## Location

**San Francisco** or **New York City** — two sites, both with **on-site laboratory space** included. In-person program.

## Money

- **Guaranteed (on acceptance):** **$250,000** initial investment for ~**8% equity**.
- **Not guaranteed:** staged toward **~$525,000 total (~10%)** — the additional ~$275K is milestone-/next-round follow-on, gotten through program progress and the SOSV fund's continued investment, not automatic.
- **Equity / IP:** dilutive (~8% → ~10%); no IP claim.

## Deadlines & decision

- **Rolling** — no fixed deadline.
- **Decision:** rolling review.

## Format

- **"Camp."** ~**4-month in-person program** in SF or NYC, with lab access, intensive mentorship, and an investor network; ends in an investor showcase / demo day.
- **Move?** Yes, temporarily — relocate to SF or NYC for ~4 months.
- **Help + money, both, with wet-lab resources** — the pool historically skews wetlab/synbio, so our "AI-is-the-instrument" thesis has to carry the pitch rather than the chemistry.

## Selectivity (rounds · applicants · acceptees)

- **Rounds/year:** 2 cohorts per location (SF and NY) → up to 4 cohorts/yr across both sites.
- **Applicants/round:** not publicly disclosed; selective.
- **Accepted/round:** ~15 companies (~30/location/yr).

## What's in it for them

Same SOSV fund model as [[HAX (SOSV)]] — equity returns on a deep-tech/bio portfolio. The included wet lab is a de-risking tool: getting a biology startup to a hard data milestone fast raises both the odds and the valuation of the next round SOSV co-invests in. The framing is mission-flavored (human and planetary health), but financially it is a venture fund making early bets.

## Fit for ECSEQ

Natural fit as a **biotech instrument** company, and the included lab space is directly useful for the electrochemistry/wet-lab side of [[Risks & Kill-Criteria|validation]]. Rolling → **apply post-pixel**. The main caveat: the cohort leans biology, so lead with the platform/AI framing from the [[Business Plan]] and [[Throughput & Competitive Landscape]].
