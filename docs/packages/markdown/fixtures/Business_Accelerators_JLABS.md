# JLABS

Status: DRAFT — 2026-07-25. Johnson & Johnson's "no strings attached" life-science incubator. Part of [[Accelerators]]; see [[Funding]]. Sources accessed 2026-07-25: [JLABS residency application](https://jlabsresidency.smapply.io/), [no-strings model](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/tech/jlabs-executive-roi-a-no-strings-attached-health-tech-incubator).

**One line:** no capital at all — lab space, equipment, and J&J network with **no equity and no IP claim**. A later-stage validation/partnership play, not a funding source.

## Location

Multiple hubs — **South San Francisco, San Diego, Boston, Houston, Washington DC, Toronto** and others (the NYC site closed in 2025). You reside at whichever JLABS location you're admitted to.

## Money

- **Guaranteed:** **none.** JLABS provides **no funding** — its offering is subsidized **wet-lab and dry-desk space, equipment, education, mentorship (JPALS), and access to J&J experts and investor relationships**.
- **Not guaranteed:** no direct capital at any stage; the upside is that residency can lead to partnership/investment conversations with J&J or its network, but nothing is committed.
- **Equity / IP:** **none taken.** J&J takes no equity, no IP rights, and residents aren't bound to partner with J&J after residency. Genuinely no-strings.

## Deadlines & decision

- **Rolling** application via the online residency portal.
- **Decision:** reviewed against selection criteria (transformational science, unmet medical/market need, strong team, financial solvency, strategic-adjacency fit); no fixed public SLA. Requires a 12–18 month project plan, IP/license background, and team bios.

## Format

- **"From now on," long residency** (~2-year average) — lab space + network, **help not money**.
- **Move?** Yes — you operate out of a JLABS facility, but it's a tenancy, not a time-boxed camp.
- **JUST help (space + network), zero money** — the inverse of a cash-only program.

## Selectivity (rounds · applicants · acceptees)

- **Rounds/year:** none — **rolling, continuous** admission as lab space opens.
- **Applicants/round:** not publicly disclosed.
- **Accepted:** capacity ~**225 resident companies** across ~6 sites; admits continuously rather than in batches.

## What's in it for them

J&J runs JLABS as **strategic corporate venture scouting without the equity**. By giving promising life-science startups subsidized lab space and network access, J&J buys an **early, first-look pipeline** for future partnerships, licensing, and acquisitions — plus ecosystem goodwill and full utilization of premium lab space. It deliberately takes no equity or IP because the payoff it wants is *optionality and proximity* to innovation it might later partner with or buy, not a fund return. You cost them space; they get a front-row seat.

## Fit for ECSEQ

Not a capital source, so it doesn't help the near-term de-risking problem. Its value is **later**: once [[Risks & Kill-Criteria|single-pixel]] and chip data exist, subsidized lab space and a J&J diagnostics/med-device network become a real partnership and scale-up asset. Park it as a post-validation option, ranked last here purely because it provides no funding — not because the resources are weak.
