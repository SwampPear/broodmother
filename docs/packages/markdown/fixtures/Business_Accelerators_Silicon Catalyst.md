# Silicon Catalyst

Status: DRAFT — 2026-07-25. The only incubator built specifically around semiconductor startups. Part of [[Accelerators]]; see [[Funding]]. Sources accessed 2026-07-25: [Silicon Catalyst](https://siliconcatalyst.com/), [Silicon Catalyst Ventures](https://siliconcatalyst.com/silicon-catalyst-ventures-launched-to-support-earlystage-semiconductor-startups), [apply](https://siliconcatalyst.com/application).

**One line:** low cash, but uniquely relevant in-kind (EDA tools, foundry/IP access, patent/legal support) and a semiconductor-native investor base — the specialist option for the fab side.

## Location

**Silicon Valley** (San Jose / Santa Clara area) HQ, with global reach (Silicon Catalyst UK, EU, Japan, Israel). The core program is **mostly virtual + partner-network access** rather than a single-building residency.

## Money

- **Guaranteed (on admission):** most seed-stage companies are eligible for **~$150,000** on admission. The larger value is **in-kind**: EDA tool licenses, foundry / IP-block partner access, and legal/patent support — the expensive infrastructure a chip startup otherwise can't afford.
- **Not guaranteed:** up to **$2M in follow-on** (competitive), plus investment from **Silicon Catalyst Ventures** (a ~$10–20M/series fund for admitted companies) and access to **Silicon Catalyst Angels**. All selective — gotten by competing, milestone progress, and the affiliated fund electing to invest.
- **Equity / IP:** does **not** typically take a large equity stake up front; the model is long-term relationships + selective investment via affiliated vehicles. Confirm exact terms directly.

## Deadlines & decision

- **Rolling** in the US (regional cohorts like ChipStart EU run on their own fixed dates — e.g. EU Cohort 2 closed Mar 1, 2026 — but those are region-specific).
- **Decision:** application → screening call → ecosystem committee review; **no fixed public SLA**.

## Format

- **"From now on," ~24-month** customized incubation — long-horizon, mostly **help from afar** (virtual + partner introductions) rather than a relocate-and-camp model.
- **Move?** Largely no — it's distributed/virtual with partner access.
- **More help than money** — the point is the semiconductor infrastructure (tools, foundry, IP, expertise) and investor connections, not a large check.

## Selectivity (rounds · applicants · acceptees)

- **Rounds/year:** rolling, with periodic cohort admissions (~3–7 companies per announcement, several per year).
- **Applicants/round:** **1,500+** semiconductor startups engaged worldwide; no fixed per-round applicant count published.
- **Accepted:** **~150 admitted total** since 2015 (~15–20/yr) — highly selective.

## What's in it for them

A **two-sided model**. The high-value in-kind resources (EDA licenses, foundry/IP-block access) are supplied by **strategic partners** — foundries, tool vendors, and IP providers — who sponsor the incubator to build **early relationships with the next generation of chip-design customers** (today's admitted startup is tomorrow's foundry/tool account). Separately, **Silicon Catalyst Angels** and **Silicon Catalyst Ventures** take equity for financial return. So two different backers want two different things: partners want future customers, the funds want upside — and neither needs a big up-front equity bite, which is why the standard admission is light on ownership.

## Fit for ECSEQ

The specialist play for our **custom-silicon / fab** needs — foundry, EDA, and IP-block access map directly onto standing up the sensor array, and the investor base actually understands semiconductor timelines. Cash is minor, so treat it as an **infrastructure + connections** program, complementary to (not a substitute for) a cash accelerator or [[Activate Fellowship|Activate]]. Best pursued once our in-house-vs-foundry [[Engineering/Fabrication/Fabrication|fab]] path is decided.
