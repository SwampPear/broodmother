# Techstars Industries of the Future

Status: DRAFT — 2026-07-25. Techstars accelerator run in partnership with Oak Ridge National Lab. Part of [[Accelerators]]; see [[Funding]]. Sources accessed 2026-07-25: [ORNL announcement](https://www.ornl.gov/news/application-techstars-industries-future-accelerator-opens), [Techstars deadlines](https://zyner.io/blog/techstars-application-deadline). ⚠️ 2026/2027 cohort dates unconfirmed — verify before acting.

**One line:** national-lab access (ORNL/TVA/UT) is a real perk, but the terms are the generic Techstars deal and the thesis fit is broad deep-tech rather than tailored to us.

## Location

**Knoxville / Oak Ridge, Tennessee**, in partnership with **Oak Ridge National Laboratory (DOE)**, the **Tennessee Valley Authority**, and the **University of Tennessee System**. In-person program.

## Money

- **Guaranteed (at program start):** the **standard Techstars deal** — commonly structured as **~$20,000 for ~6% common equity + a ~$100,000 convertible note ≈ ~$120,000 total**.
- **Not guaranteed:** the convertible-note portion converts at a later priced round (standard note mechanics); beyond that, value is access to ORNL facilities/scientists and the Techstars investor network at demo day, plus whatever follow-on the network catalyzes.
- **Equity / IP:** dilutive (~6% + note); no IP claim.

## Deadlines & decision

- Cohorts of **~10 hard-tech startups** (AI, advanced manufacturing, quantum, advanced wireless, biotech, clean energy).
- **Dates unconfirmed for the current cycle** — the last well-documented cohort's deadline was **Nov 2023** (for a 2024 cohort). Check apply.techstars.com and the ORNL page for the live window before planning around it.
- **Decision:** standard Techstars timeline — interviews, then selection a few weeks after the deadline.

## Format

- **"Camp."** 3-month, in-person, cohort accelerator on standard Techstars structure, ending in a demo day.
- **Move?** Yes, temporarily — relocate to the Knoxville/Oak Ridge area for ~3 months.
- **Help + money, both**, with the distinctive asset being **DOE national-lab access** (ORNL instrumentation and scientists).

## Selectivity (rounds · applicants · acceptees)

- **Rounds/year:** ~1 cohort/year for this ORNL program.
- **Applicants/round:** hundreds to thousands per program.
- **Accepted/round:** ~10–12 startups; acceptance **~1–2% (often <1%)**.

## What's in it for them

Two motives stacked. **Techstars is a VC** — the ~6% + convertible note across its very large global portfolio is the financial engine, power-law like YC but with smaller checks and wider reach. And the **program partners (ORNL, TVA, University of Tennessee) sponsor** this specific accelerator to get an **innovation and tech-transfer pipeline** — early access to startups working near their research plus regional economic-development goals. You are simultaneously a portfolio bet for Techstars and a pipeline input for the national-lab partners.

## Fit for ECSEQ

The ORNL tie-in is genuinely interesting for a hardware/materials company — national-lab instrumentation could support characterization. But the deal is smaller and the program's remit is generic deep-tech, so the fit is weaker than the hardware/bio specialists ([[HAX (SOSV)]], [[SOSV SF & NY (IndieBio)]]). Consider mainly if the ORNL facility access maps onto a specific [[Engineering/Fabrication/Fabrication|fab]] or characterization need; otherwise deprioritize.
