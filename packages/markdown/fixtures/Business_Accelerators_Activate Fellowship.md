# Activate Fellowship

Status: DRAFT — 2026-07-25. Non-dilutive hard-tech fellowship. Part of [[Accelerators]]; see [[Funding]] for where it sits in the plan. Sources accessed 2026-07-25: [The Fellowship](https://activate.org/the-fellowship), [FAQ](https://activate.org/faq), [apply](https://activate.org/apply).

**One line:** the best financial *and* strategic fit — real 2-year runway, takes **no equity and no IP**, but you commit to a host lab and must be a first-time hard-tech founder.

## Location

Host-lab based, not a single campus. Programs run out of **Berkeley, Boston, Houston, and New York**, plus **"Activate Anywhere"** — a remote track for fellows who stay at their own institution. You anchor to a host lab / metro for two years but this is not a relocate-to-one-building model.

## Money

- **Guaranteed (once selected):** **≥ $350,000 in direct support over 2 years** — a living stipend of **$80K–$110K/yr**, plus health-insurance stipend and travel allowance, plus **$100,000 of research funding** at the host laboratory.
- **Not guaranteed:** the "additional flexible capital" folded into that $350K+ figure is allocated at Activate's discretion; the stipend + $100K research core is the reliable floor. There is no large conditional follow-on tranche the way equity accelerators have — the value beyond cash is resources, lab access, and network.
- **Equity / IP:** **none.** All support is a grant; Activate takes no equity and no rights to IP. Fellows retain ownership. Funded via DOE, DOD, and philanthropies.

## Format

- **"From now on," 2 years.** Not a camp. You keep running *your* company with the stipend, research funds, resources, and network attached — closer to a funded fellowship than a time-boxed bootcamp.
- **Help + money, heavily both.** Cash floor *and* leadership development (workshops, trainings), 1:1s with community team, and a scientist/investor/operator network.
- **Move?** Depends on track — host-lab tracks anchor you to that metro; "Activate Anywhere" lets you stay put. Temporary (2-yr term), not permanent.

## Selectivity (rounds · applicants · acceptees)

- **Rounds/year:** 1 cohort/year.
- **Applicants/round:** ~900–1,040 (Cohort 2024: 1,040; Cohort 2025: 900+).
- **Accepted/round:** ~47–62 fellows (~50 companies) → **~5–9% acceptance** (296 fellows to date, ~8.8% lifetime).

## What's in it for them

Activate is a **nonprofit**, funded by the **DOE, DOD, and philanthropies**. It takes no equity and no IP because it is not chasing a financial return — its mandate is to move hard science out of the lab into commercial and public benefit (energy, climate, national competitiveness). Its "return" is impact, measured and reported to those government and philanthropic backers, which is exactly why the money is a true grant. Selection favors science with large societal/economic upside plus a first-time founder who needs precisely this runway — so the fit case is about *mission alignment*, not exit potential.
