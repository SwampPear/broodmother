# Y Combinator

Status: DRAFT — 2026-07-25. The best-known startup accelerator; batch-based, equity. Part of [[Accelerators]]; see [[Funding]]. Sources accessed 2026-07-25: [apply](https://ycombinator.com/apply), [the $500K deal](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/ycs-500-000-standard-deal), [the deal terms](https://ycombinator.com/deal).

**One line:** unmatched brand, network, and demo-day fundraising lift — but the least patient with multi-year fab/validation timelines. Target Winter 2027, not Fall 2026.

## Location

**San Francisco.** The batch is **in person** in the Bay Area for its full 3-month duration.

## Money

- **Guaranteed (on acceptance — all accepted companies get the full amount):** **$500,000**, in two SAFEs:
  - **$125,000 for 7%** (post-money SAFE, fixed equity).
  - **$375,000 on an uncapped MFN SAFE** — no valuation cap of its own; later adopts the most-favorable terms of any SAFE issued before the next priced round. See [[Funding]] for the mechanics.
- **Not guaranteed:** the $375K is **not** milestone-gated — the only real gate is **getting in (~1% of applicants)**. Separately, YC's later-stage follow-on / pro-rata investment in future rounds *is* selective, but that's downstream of the batch.
- **Equity / IP:** 7% fixed + a variable amount from the $375K SAFE when it converts; no IP claim.

## Deadlines & decision

- YC now runs **four batches/year** (Winter, Spring, Summer, Fall), each 3 months in SF.
- **Fall 2026:** deadline **July 27, 2026, 8pm PT**; decision by **Aug 28, 2026**; batch Oct–Dec 2026. *(Too soon and pre-pixel for us.)*
- **Winter 2027:** ~Nov 2026 deadline (projected, not yet officially confirmed). **Early Decision** is open now to apply ahead for Winter/Spring/Summer 2027.
- **Decision:** roughly **~1 month** after the deadline.

## Format

- **"Camp."** 3-month, in-person, cohort batch in SF, ending in Demo Day where you pitch a room of investors.
- **Move?** Yes, temporarily — relocate to the Bay Area for the 3 months.
- **Help + money, both, but the standout is the network + fundraising signal**, not the cash. A YC batch materially accelerates the next raise via brand and demo-day access.

## Selectivity (rounds · applicants · acceptees)

- **Rounds/year:** 4 (Winter, Spring, Summer, Fall).
- **Applicants/round:** ~20,000–40,000.
- **Accepted/round:** ~150–250 companies (Winter 2026: 199; Spring 2026: 193) → ~600–800/yr. Acceptance **~0.6–1.5%** (Summer 2025 hit a record-low ~0.6%).

## What's in it for them

YC is a **venture fund**. The $500K buys 7%+ of every company, and across ~5,000 alumni the returns follow a power law — a few outcomes (Airbnb, Stripe, Coinbase, DoorDash, Dropbox) pay for the entire portfolio. That's why it can run a ~1% acceptance rate and hand out non-milestone money: it's placing a large number of cheap, diversified early bets and needs only a handful to become enormous. In their model you are a low-cost option with asymmetric upside.

## Fit for ECSEQ

Best brand/network of any option, and the ~60%-AI recent batches reward the "AI is the instrument" framing. But YC is impatient with hardware/fab/validation timelines — this is a **bet on narrative**. **Do not apply Fall 2026** (pre-pixel, rushed against the [[Patent Plan|provisional filing]]); **target Winter 2027**, by which point [[Risks & Kill-Criteria|single-pixel]] data transforms the pitch from "does the physics work" to "can we scale."
