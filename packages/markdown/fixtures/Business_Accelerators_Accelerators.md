## What these are

An **accelerator** is a fixed-term program that gives a startup a modest amount of money plus mentorship, network, and (sometimes) lab space, usually in exchange for **equity** (dilutive). A **fellowship / incubator** (Activate, The Engine's Blueprint, JLABS) gives money and/or resources with **no equity taken** (non-dilutive). We evaluate both here because the decision axis that matters to us — *what we give up vs. what we get* — cuts across the label.

See [[Funding]] for how these sit inside the broader plan: **non-dilutive first (grants/fellowships), dilutive accelerators after the single-pixel validation** ([[Risks & Kill-Criteria]]), when a validated result makes equity cheap.

## The options (sub-pages)

- [[Activate Fellowship]] — non-dilutive, ≥$350K over 2 yrs, **0% equity, no IP claim**; 2-yr host-lab fellowship. Best financial + strategic fit.
- [[HAX (SOSV)]] — up to ~$550K, ~10% equity; ~6-mo hardware residency (Newark). Strongest *hardware/chip* accelerator fit.
- [[SOSV SF & NY (IndieBio)]] — up to ~$525K, ~8–10% equity; ~4-mo residency with lab access (SF/NYC). Strongest *biotech-instrument* accelerator fit.
- [[Y Combinator]] — $500K, 7%+; 3-mo batch (SF). Best brand, network, and demo-day fundraising lift.
- [[Silicon Catalyst]] — ~$150K + heavy in-kind (EDA/foundry/IP), up to $2M follow-on; 24-mo incubation, mostly virtual. Semiconductor-native.
- [[The Engine]] — Blueprint: no-cost, no-equity translational accelerator; Engine Ventures invests separately. Cambridge, MA / tough-tech patient.
- [[Techstars Industries of the Future]] — ~$120K standard Techstars deal, ~6% + note; 3-mo camp (Oak Ridge/Knoxville, TN). National-lab tie-in.
- [[JLABS]] — no money, no equity, no IP claim; lab space + J&J network. Later-stage partnership play.

## Ranking (best → worst for us), 2026-07-25

Weighs: money we get · equity/IP we give · applicability to a biointegrated-semiconductor + AI + bio company · general brand/network quality. Full reasoning in chat and per sub-page.

1. **[[Activate Fellowship]]** — non-dilutive, real 2-yr runway, keeps 100% + IP. Apply *now* (pre-pixel). Gated on first-time-hard-tech-founder eligibility.
2. **[[HAX (SOSV)]]** — most money among equity options, hardware-native, rolling → apply post-pixel with no deadline pressure.
3. **[[SOSV SF & NY (IndieBio)]]** — biotech-instrument narrative + wet lab, rolling → post-pixel.
4. **[[Y Combinator]]** — unmatched brand/fundraising lift; least patient with fab timelines. Target Winter 2027, not Fall 2026.
5. **[[Silicon Catalyst]]** — low cash but uniquely relevant fab/EDA/foundry in-kind + semiconductor investors.
6. **[[The Engine]]** — patient tough-tech capital + MIT ecosystem; Blueprint is help-not-money, Engine Ventures is a later equity path.
7. **[[Techstars Industries of the Future]]** — national-lab access is a real perk, but standard terms are less generous and the fit is generic deep-tech.
8. **[[JLABS]]** — no capital; a validation/partnership play once chip data exists, not a funding source.
