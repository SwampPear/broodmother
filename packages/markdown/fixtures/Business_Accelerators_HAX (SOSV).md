# HAX (SOSV)

Status: DRAFT — 2026-07-25. Hard-tech accelerator, the hardware/robotics arm of SOSV. Part of [[Accelerators]]; see [[Funding]]. Sources accessed 2026-07-25: [HAX program](https://hax.co/program/), [apply](https://hax.co/apply/accelerator/).

**One line:** the strongest *hardware/chip* accelerator fit and the most money among the equity options — rolling admission means we can apply the week single-pixel data lands.

## Location

**Newark, New Jersey** HQ (SOSV's hard-tech hub), with additional SOSV presence in Shenzhen, Pune, and Tokyo. The program is an **in-person residency** at the Newark facility.

## Money

- **Guaranteed (on acceptance):** up to **$250,000** initial investment for ~**10% equity**, on standard SOSV terms.
- **Not guaranteed:** staged toward **up to ~$550,000 total**, combining cash follow-on and **in-kind** support (engineering, prototyping, supply-chain). The extra beyond the first $250K is gotten by **hitting program milestones**, the **next priced round** (SOSV's fund can invest pro-rata), and continued participation — not automatic.
- **Equity / IP:** dilutive (~10% for the package); no IP claim.

## Deadlines & decision

- **Rolling** — no fixed batch deadline. Apply anytime.
- **Decision:** rolling — application → screening → interviews, typically a few weeks.

## Format

- **"Camp."** ~**6-month in-person residency** in Newark, hands-on with a dedicated team of engineers, designers, and business staff — built specifically for physical-product / hardware startups.
- **Move?** Yes, temporarily — you relocate to Newark for the ~6-month residency.
- **Help + money, both, and unusually deep on the engineering/prototyping side** — this is the program that best understands building physical devices and getting to manufacturing.

## Selectivity (rounds · applicants · acceptees)

- **Rounds/year:** ~2 cohorts (each a 180-day residency).
- **Applicants/round:** not publicly disclosed; SOSV programs are highly selective.
- **Accepted:** HAX funds **~35 startups/year** (~15–18/cohort). SOSV overall backs ~150 new startups/yr across HAX, IndieBio, and Orbit.

## What's in it for them

SOSV is a **deep-tech VC fund** (raised a $277M fourth fund). HAX is its hardware engine: the ~10% stake across a large hard-tech portfolio is the return mechanism, and the in-house engineering/prototyping team exists to de-risk those bets faster and cheaper than the startups could alone. They win when a portfolio company reaches a priced round at a much higher valuation — so their incentives align with pushing you to a fundable manufacturing milestone.

## Fit for ECSEQ

Excellent for the chip/sensor-array side — HAX is oriented around exactly the "build a physical device and prepare for production" problem we face. Rolling admission is a gift: **apply post-pixel** ([[Risks & Kill-Criteria]]), with no deadline pressure, when the validated result maximizes valuation. Requires ≥2 complementary co-founders and a credible prototype/roadmap — aligns with closing our [[Business Plan|team gap]].
