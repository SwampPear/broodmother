# Funding

# Grant Capital

- funds work *before *validation
- NSF SBIR Phase I exists specifically to pay down technical risk
- Congress reauthorized SBIR/STTR in April 2026 through September 2031, ending the early-2026 pause, and NSF reopened Project Pitch submissions on June 2, 2026
- \$250M restart includes a new \$40M pilot for scientific instrumentation under solicitation NSF 26-511
- That lane is a stronger fit for ECSEQ-1 than the standard 26-510 track, because the reviewer is asking whether the instrument opens a class of measurement that isn't otherwise accessible rather than just whether it's deep tech with a market.
- Wash-free electrochemical base discrimination is exactly that framing: a new measurement modality, not a cheaper version of an existing box
- [Granted AI](https://grantedai.com/news/nsf-sbir-sttr-250m-2026-05-31)
- Phase I awards run up to \$305,000 for 6 to 18 months, with full-proposal deadlines of July 27, 2026, November 4, 2026, and March 4, 2027
- The pitch is the on-ramp and you hear back in about one to two months
- July 27 is realistically too tight unless you already hold an invitation, so November 4 is live target
- Note one new constraint: enhanced foreign-influence and beneficial-ownership disclosure is now required across all agencies, and pitches are capped at two per company per year. [Granted AI](https://grantedai.com/news/nsf-sbir-sttr-250m-2026-05-31)

# Activate

- will fund credible pre-validation concept
- It will fund a credible pre-validation concept, but the bar is higher than SBIR
- They'll consider early concepts that aren't fully validated if backed by convincing cost or technoeconomic modeling, though most applicants have an early proof of concept supported by data showing the innovation works
- Single-pixel data wouldn't be strictly required, but it would move you from the "convincing modeling" tier to the "demonstrated" tier, which is a real difference in a competitive pool
- Cohort 2027 recruitment is live now with webinars running and an interest form open, and the program provides more than \$300K over two years for stipend and R&D plus \$100,000 in research funding per project. Worth confirming you fit the first-time-hard-tech-founder requirement [Activate](https://activate.org/faq)

# Angel Capital

- gates on single-pixel validation
- An equity check at pre-seed is a bet on your core thesis, and your entire thesis lives or dies on whether real incorporation events produce distinguishable impedance signatures on the Pt/Au stack. Before that result, an angel is underwriting a simulation and a fabrication plan
- After it, the technical risk profile changes categorically and the conversation shifts from "does the physics work" to "can you scale the array and yield." That's the milestone that converts a maybe into a term sheet on the equity side.

# Accelerators

Ranked by fit for a biointegrated semiconductor company (hardware + AI + biotech), not by prestige. [[Business Plan]]

## YC

- batch deadlines, not rolling: Fall 2026 (F26) closes July 27, 2026 8pm PT, decision by Aug 28 — next window is Winter 2027 (~Nov 2026 deadline)
- funds on acceptance, before the batch even starts — fastest cash-in-hand of any program here
- 3-month in-person program, SF; ~150-200 companies/batch out of 10k+ applicants
- 2026 batches are reportedly ~60% AI-heavy, which plays to the "AI is the primary instrument" framing better than a pure hardware pitch would
- less patient with multi-year fab/validation timelines than the deep-tech specialists below — this is a bet on narrative more than domain fit
- [YC Application Deadline Dates](https://zyner.io/blog/yc-application-deadline)

## HAX (SOSV)

- rolling applications, no fixed deadline
- 6-month residency, Newark HQ (plus Shenzhen/Pune/Tokyo)
- up to \$550K total: \$150K cash + \$100K in-kind upfront at acceptance
- hardware/robotics-focused sister program to IndieBio under SOSV — better fit for the chip/sensor-array side than a straight biotech pitch
- [Apply to HAX](https://hax.co/apply/accelerator/)

## IndieBio / SOSV SF & SOSV NY

- rebranded from IndieBio to SOSV SF / SOSV NY in early 2026; same program, broader deep-tech remit
- rolling applications, no fixed deadline
- 4-5 month program, SF or NYC, lab access included
- \$250K initial for 8% equity at acceptance, staged toward \$525K total (~10%)
- most natural narrative fit for ECSEQ-1 as a biotech instrument, though the pool skews wetlab/synbio more than chip fab — the AI-instrument thesis needs to carry the pitch
- [IndieBio is Now SOSV NY & SOSV SF](https://sosv.com/indiebio-is-now-sosv-ny-sosv-sf-underscoring-sosvs-leadership-in-deep-tech/)

## Silicon Catalyst

- rolling in the US; Silicon Catalyst Japan cohort one starts Jan 2026
- application → screening call → committee review, no fixed public SLA
- 24-month customized incubation
- mostly in-kind (EDA tools, foundry/IP partner access, legal/patent support), not cash — confirm the cash component directly, search didn't surface it
- only accelerator here literally built around semiconductor startups — relevant given the custom silicon at this stage
- [Silicon Catalyst — Submit Application](https://siliconcatalyst.com/application)

## Techstars Industries of the Future

- partnership with Oak Ridge National Lab, TVA, University of Tennessee; ~10 startups/cohort across AI, advanced manufacturing, biotech, clean energy
- Spring 2026 cohort closed (deadline was Nov 29, 2025); Fall 2026 cohort applications projected to open ~March 2026, close ~June 2026
- 3-month program, standard Techstars terms (~\$120K) at program start
- [Techstars Industries of the Future Accelerator | ORNL](https://www.ornl.gov/news/application-techstars-industries-future-accelerator-opens)

## The Engine (MIT)

- "tough tech" incubator/VC hybrid, comfortable with multi-year fab and physical-science timelines
- no fixed public deadline found — appears rolling/direct-outreach rather than batch deadlines; investment terms negotiated per-company, not disclosed
- worth a direct email rather than relying on secondhand sources
- [The Home for Tough Tech](https://engine.xyz/)

## JLABS (J&J)

- rolling application via online portal
- no-equity, no-IP-claim model — but also **no funding**; value is lab space and partnership access, ~2-year average residency
- later-stage validation/partnership play once chip data exists, not a capital source
- [Application Process | JNJ Innovation](https://jnjinnovation.com/jlabs/apply)
